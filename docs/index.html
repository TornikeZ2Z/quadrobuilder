How this was built, and what to distrust
  • Five Optimo Excel exports loaded into DuckDB, then embedded row-level so every filter recalculates in the browser.
  • Revenue matches Optimo exactly — 29,944.80 ₾ over the full period, difference 0.00. Revenue counts retail lines excluding those flagged returned, plus B2B lines excluding those flagged cancelled. Cancelled B2B lines are dropped at load and appear nowhere on this page.
  • Margin is approximate. Optimo's exports carry no cost-at-time-of-sale, so margin uses each product's current purchase price, and will differ from Optimo's own markup figure. 7 B2B lines (269 ₾) have no cost record at all and count as pure margin, slightly flattering the total.
  • Velocity, cover and overstock follow the selected period. Products with fewer than 3 sales are excluded from the reorder list so a single sale cannot look like a trend.
