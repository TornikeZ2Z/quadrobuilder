How this was built
  • Five Excel exports from Optimo — retail line items, B2B line items, stock on hand, stock movement, daily statistics — loaded into DuckDB, then embedded row-level so every filter recalculates in the browser.
  • Returns (დაბრუნებული) are subtracted, not summed. Optimo's own dashboard adds them to revenue instead, which is why its headline is higher than the one here.
  • Margin is approximate. Optimo's export carries no cost-at-time-of-sale, so margin uses each product's current purchase price. Roughly 29% here against Optimo's own 24% is the size of that gap — directionally sound, not exact to the tetri.
  • Velocity and days-of-cover follow the selected period. Products with fewer than 3 sales are excluded from velocity ranking — with 372 products across 909 lines, a single sale would otherwise look like a trend.
